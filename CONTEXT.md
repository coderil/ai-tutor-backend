# AI Tutor

A learning companion that teaches a subject one lesson at a time, remembering what the
learner has already shown they can do and choosing each next lesson to sit just beyond it.

## Language

**Workspace**:
The container for one learner's study of one subject. Holds the mission, sources, lessons,
learning records, glossary and reference documents.
_Avoid_: Course, Project, Folder

**Mission**:
Why the learner wants to learn the subject, what success looks like, and what is out of
scope. Every lesson is chosen against it.
_Avoid_: Goal, Objective

**Source**:
A resource the learner's lessons draw their claims from, kept alongside the learner's own
note on why it is worth reading.
_Avoid_: Resource, Reference, Link

**Gap**:
An explicit record that the mission needs something no good source covers. Named so its
absence stays visible instead of silent.
_Avoid_: Missing, Unknown

**Lesson**:
One self-contained teaching unit covering a single skill, short enough to finish in a
single sitting.
_Avoid_: Chapter, Session, Module

**Lesson Attempt**:
A learner's answers to a lesson's questions, together with the grading that came back.
_Avoid_: Submission, Response, Quiz attempt

**Learning Record**:
An evidence-backed note of something the learner has demonstrated understanding of, or a
misconception they have had corrected.
_Avoid_: Progress, Log, Session note, History

**Superseded**:
A learning record contradicted by later evidence. It stays in place, marked, and is never
deleted.
_Avoid_: Deleted, Replaced, Obsolete

**Glossary Term**:
A word the learner has used correctly, carrying the one word to prefer and the aliases to
avoid.
_Avoid_: Vocabulary, Keywords

**Reference Document**:
The compressed essence of a lesson's material, kept for quick lookup long after the
lesson is done.
_Avoid_: Summary, Cheatsheet, Notes

**Zone of Proximal Development**:
The band of skill just beyond what the learner can currently do unaided. Where the next
lesson is aimed.
_Avoid_: Difficulty level, Level, Skill level

**Community**:
A real place, online or offline, where the learner tests what they has learned against
others.
_Avoid_: Forum, Social
